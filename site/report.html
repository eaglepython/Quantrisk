Reading this section

  • Effective duration comes from repricing the bond with the whole curve shifted up and down 1bp. For MBS it is lower than modified duration because homeowners prepay when rates fall.
  • Negative convexity on the MBS pools means their price gains shrink as rates fall and losses grow as rates rise.
  • Key-rate DV01 splits interest-rate risk by curve point; the four bars add up to total DV01 ($267,714 per bp).
